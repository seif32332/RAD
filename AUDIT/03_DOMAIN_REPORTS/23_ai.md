# 23 AI / decision intelligence

Group H audited specialists 19, 20 and 23 in one report: [19_workflow_notifications_ai.md](19_workflow_notifications_ai.md). See the sections "AI / LLM integrations", "ML component (face service)" and "Workforce decision support (rules.ts / reasons.ts)", the scorecard row "23 AI / decision intelligence", and finding H-8.

Summary: no LLM, agent, prompt or model-routing code exists anywhere (EV-8028, EV-8029, EV-0018). This is a deliberate policy (DEC-006), enforced by the CI job `ai-processors`, not an unfinished feature. The only ML component is the on-premise face service (classical computer vision: detection, liveness, embedding) used for self clock-in (EV-0017). Workforce "recommendations" and "reasons" are deterministic lookups against the dated, sourced `RuleParameter` table and static legal-citation tables (EV-8032, EV-8033). They are decision support, not AI, and should not be marketed as AI. The audit dimensions for AI (hallucination controls, citations, human approval, cost, fallback) are therefore not applicable, not failed.
